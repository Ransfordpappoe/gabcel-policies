Account Deletion Request

Thank you for using the Gabcel App. We respect your privacy and your right to have your account and associated data deleted from our systems. This page explains how to request account deletion and what data will be affected.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Gabcel App account, please follow these simple steps:

  1. Send an Email: Contact our support team at bethelsoftwareteam@gamil.com
  2. Include Your Identity Information: Provide the following details in your request to verify your identity:
    • Full name associated with your Gabcel account
    • Email address used for your account registration
    • Phone number linked to your account
    • National ID or any unique identifier we have on file
  3. Confirm Your Request: Clearly state: "I request to have my Gabcel App account completely deleted along with my personal data."
  4. Await Confirmation: Our team will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.

✓ Email Address for Deletion Requests

bethelsoftwareteam@gamil.com

Please include "[DELETE ACCOUNT REQUEST]" in your email subject line.

Data Deletion Policy

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your personal profile information and account credentials
  • Email address and contact information
  • Phone number and authentication records
  • National ID and identity verification documents
  • Transaction history and hire purchase agreement details specific to your account
  • Payment information and financial records
  • GPS location history and tracking data
  • Device information and device registration records
  • User preferences and app settings
  • All uploaded documents and media associated with your account

Data Retention After Deletion

The following information may be retained for legal, regulatory, and business continuity purposes:

  • Legal and Financial Records: Transaction receipts, payment proofs, and tax documentation required by law (retained for 7 years to comply with financial regulations)
  • Compliance and Fraud Prevention: Anonymized records used to detect and prevent fraudulent activities and abuse (retained for 12 months)
  • Customer Support Logs: De-identified support interactions and correspondence (retained for 2 years for service improvement)
  • Backup Systems: Data may exist in backup storage systems for operational resilience and will be deleted after the standard backup retention period (typically 90 days)
  • Aggregated Analytics: Anonymous, aggregated data that cannot identify you (retained indefinitely for analytics)

Processing Timeline

  • Verification: 1-3 business days (we will verify your identity)
  • Processing: 5-10 business days (we will delete all applicable personal data)
  • Confirmation: You will receive an email confirming that your account and data have been deleted
  • Total Expected Timeline: 30 days from submission

⚠ Important Information

Account Reactivation: After deletion, you will not be able to reactivate or recover your deleted account and associated data. If you wish to use the Gabcel App again, you will need to create a new account.

Third-Party Data Removal: If your data has been shared with third parties (such as hire purchase companies, verification services, or payment processors) as part of normal operations, we will notify them to delete your data. However, their independent data retention policies may apply.

Active Transactions: If you have active hire purchase agreements, your account may not be immediately deleted as we need to retain records for financial and legal compliance. Please contact us to discuss your specific situation.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about the account deletion process or wish to discuss your data privacy rights, please contact us:

  • Subject Line: Use "[DATA REQUEST]" for general inquiries or "[DELETE ACCOUNT REQUEST]" for deletion requests
