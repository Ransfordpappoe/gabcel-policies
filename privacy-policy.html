4. GPS Location & Timestamp Tracking

Important Notice: The Gabcel App uses GPS location and precise timestamps for security and accountability purposes.

4.1 Purpose of Location Tracking

  • Verify that transactions occur at the stated location
  • Detect unauthorized or fraudulent transactions
  • Manage field agent accountability and performance
  • Prevent data manipulation and off-book transactions
  • Enhance data accuracy for hire purchase records

4.2 Location Data Usage

GPS coordinates and timestamps are:

  • Automatically recorded with every transaction entry
  • Stored in the central database linked to your user account
  • Used to verify agent activity and transaction legitimacy
  • Included in performance analytics and reports
  • Available for investigation of disputed transactions

4.3 Your Responsibilities

  • You must not attempt to bypass, spoof, or manipulate GPS data
  • Do not use location spoofing apps or services while using Gabcel
  • Ensure your device's location services are enabled and accurate
  • Accurate location data is a requirement of your role as a Gabcel representative

5. National ID & Identity Information

Sensitive Data: National ID numbers and identification information are critical to our hire purchase operations and are treated with the highest security standards.

5.1 Collection & Verification

  • National ID information is collected to verify customer eligibility for hire purchase agreements
  • All agents must verify the authenticity of identification before recording
  • Incorrect or fraudulent identification must be reported immediately

5.2 Data Protection for Identity Information

  • National ID numbers are encrypted during transmission and at rest
  • Access to identity information is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Manual collection of ID information must be verified before saving
  • Duplicate checking is performed to prevent identity fraud

5.3 Data Retention for Identity Information

Customer identity information is retained for the duration of:

  • The active hire purchase agreement
  • Plus applicable legal retention periods (typically 7 years for financial records)
  • Deleted upon explicit customer request (where legally permitted)

6. Payment Information & Financial Data

6.1 Payment Processing

The App processes and records:

  • Payment amounts and payment methods
  • Transaction dates and times
  • Customer payment history
  • Hire purchase terms and payment schedules

6.2 Payment Security

  • All payment transactions are encrypted using industry-standard protocols (TLS 1.2+)
  • Payment data is never stored on the mobile device in unencrypted format
  • All transactions are immediately synced to secure servers
  • We do not store complete credit card or banking credentials on the device

6.3 Real-Time Recording Requirement

  • Every payment must be recorded immediately in the App at the point of transaction
  • Manual receipts or off-book transactions are strictly prohibited
  • Failure to record transactions may result in account suspension or termination

8. Data Security & Protection

8.1 Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security protocols:

  • Encryption: TLS 1.2+ encryption for data in transit; AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Authentication: Strong password requirements and optional multi-factor authentication
  • Access Controls: Role-based access controls (RBAC) limiting data access
  • Firewalls: Advanced firewall configurations protecting infrastructure
  • Intrusion Detection: Continuous monitoring for unauthorized access attempts
  • Regular Updates: Critical security patches deployed to all devices
  • Audit Logging: Comprehensive logs of all data access and modifications

8.2 Agent Responsibilities for Security

  • Strong Passwords: Use unique, complex passwords (minimum 12 characters with mixed case, numbers, symbols)
  • No Credential Sharing: Never share your login credentials with anyone, including colleagues
  • Device Security: Keep your mobile device locked and password-protected
  • Public Wi-Fi: Avoid using unsecured Wi-Fi; use VPN when available
  • Report Compromises: Immediately notify administrators if you suspect your account is compromised
  • App Updates: Promptly install all app updates, especially security patches

8.3 Limitations

While we maintain rigorous security standards, no system is absolutely impenetrable. We cannot guarantee 100% security of all personal information. Users acknowledge the inherent risks of digital communication and data transmission.

9.2 Data Deletion
